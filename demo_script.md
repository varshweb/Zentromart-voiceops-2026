# VoiceOps 2026: 2-Minute Demo Video Walkthrough Script

This script fulfills the 2-minute video requirement specified in the Capstone Brief (Page 14):
*"A question answered from the documents with a follow-up, plus one handover or 'I don't know'"*.

---

### Segment 1: Introduction & Identity Verification (0:00 - 0:35)
* **Caller:** "Hello, I need some help with my recent order."
* **Agent (AI):** "Hello! I am ZentroMart's AI customer support assistant. I would be glad to help you. Could you please share your 8-digit order number?"
* **Caller:** "Yes, the order number is 8978 2837."
* **Agent (AI):** "For your security and identity verification, could you please provide the last 4 digits of your registered phone number or your 6-digit delivery pincode?"
* **Caller:** "The last four digits are 4 5 3 3."
* **Agent (AI):** "Verification successful, thank you Kabir! How can I assist you with order 8978 2837 today?"

---

### Segment 2: Document Grounded Question & Follow-up (0:35 - 1:15)
* **Caller:** "I bought the Kaafi Running Sneakers in this order. Can I still return them?"
* **Agent (AI):** "Yes, according to our returns policy [RET-2], fashion and footwear have a 15-day return window. Since your sneakers were delivered on 12 October, you can return them until 27 October 2026."
* **Caller (Follow-up):** "Got it. And how fast will the refund come to my wallet?"
* **Agent (AI):** "As a ZentroMart Plus member [PLS-2, RET-7], your refund is initiated at the time of pickup and will be credited to your ZentroMart Wallet instantly, within 2 hours."

---

### Segment 3: Policy Handover / Escalation (1:15 - 1:55)
* **Caller:** "I also received the Zentro Vista 43-inch 4K Smart TV in the same delivery, and the screen arrived cracked. Can you approve a replacement right now?"
* **Agent (AI):** "I am very sorry to hear that. Under ZentroMart's damage policy [DWM-3, ESC-1], replacements for high-value electronics exceeding ₹10,000 cannot be approved automatically by an AI assistant. I have created a priority support ticket for you, and our Priority Resolution team will review the damage photos and contact you within 24 hours."

---

### Segment 4: Signoff (1:55 - 2:00)
* **Caller:** "Alright, thank you for your help."
* **Agent (AI):** "You are welcome, Kabir! Thank you for choosing ZentroMart. Have a wonderful day!"
